Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 20, 2026 • Effective Date: November 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Words (“we”, “our”, or “us”), published by Auren, is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the Words mobile application (Package ID: com.auren.words) on Android devices.

Our Core Principle: Words is designed as an offline-first application. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) to external servers.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect or harvest user personal data. Here is how your data is handled:

  • No User Account: You are not required to create an account, log in, or provide your email, name, or phone number to use the app.
  • Local Database Storage: Your quote favorites, selected typography, theme choices, and widget settings are stored solely on your local device using an encrypted local SQLite database and SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device.
  • No Biometric or Location Data: We do not access or collect your GPS location, contacts, calendar, camera, or microphone.
  • No Advertising ID: We do not collect or track your Android Advertising ID (AAID).

3. Device Permissions

Words requests only standard Android permissions necessary for user-requested local functionality:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used exclusively to deliver scheduled daily motivational quotes at times you choose. You can enable or disable notifications at any time in Settings.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Used solely to re-register your local alarms and refresh your home screen widget quotes after your device restarts.

4. Third-Party Services

Words does not integrate with any third-party advertising networks, analytics tracking SDKs, or social trackers. The app utilizes standard Google Play Services solely for basic application distribution and system runtime stability provided natively by Android.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our application is suitable for general audiences aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 in accordance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international data privacy regulations.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all your data is stored locally on your device, you have complete ownership and control:

  • Clearing the app storage or cache via Android Settings immediately deletes all your stored preferences and favorites.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all associated application files from your device permanently.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
